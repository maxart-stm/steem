About Steem Wallet

Steem Wallet is an independent web wallet designed to interact with the Steem blockchain. The project was initiated by @MaxArt and developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies.

This wallet is not an official Steemit product and is not affiliated with the administrators or developers of other platforms within the Steem ecosystem.

Privacy and Security

The wallet operates in your browser, does not use cookies, and does not transmit private keys to the developer's server.

Only your account name is stored in the browser's local storage. You can view account data without a private key. For an operation requiring a signature, your Active Private Key is requested in a confirmation dialog, used locally for that operation, and not retained for subsequent operations.
